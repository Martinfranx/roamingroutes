01 — Overview
A month of structural progress

September closed with 52,485 unique accounts reached across three campaigns — 157,596 total impressions, 4,582 AED invested. The account is running its most complete setup to date: a dedicated awareness layer building consistent brand presence, a primary conversation campaign driving WhatsApp leads, and a parallel test designed to improve how Meta reads and optimises toward actual conversations.

The Traffic campaign continues to perform at exceptional efficiency — 11,088 Instagram profile visits at 0.09 AED each — keeping Roaming Routes visible to a broad UAE audience at minimal cost. Among conversation-focused activity, the Italy creative had a standout month, generating 10x more link clicks than August while maintaining a cost per click of 1.03 AED.

05 — What Worked
The signals worth building on
Creative Performance
Link Clicks by Ad — September vs August

The Italy creative broke out. Link clicks went from 34 in August to 361 in September — a 10x increase — at 1.03 AED per click. The creative is clearly connecting with the audience and has meaningful room to scale. It is now the strongest performing ad in the account.

The Traffic campaign runs at exceptional value. 11,088 Instagram profile visits at 0.09 AED each keeps Roaming Routes visible across UAE at a cost that would be difficult to match on any other channel. People who visit the profile and return to enquire days or weeks later are a real part of the funnel — this campaign builds the warm audience that everything else converts.

Link clicks to WhatsApp grew 35.6% month-over-month — from 694 in August to 941 in September. Even as formal attribution catches up, the underlying engagement is moving in the right direction.

The 25–34 audience consistently responds at the lowest cost across every campaign format. The account has found its core audience; the next step is deepening coverage within it and testing adjacent segments.

06 — October Recommendations
Where to focus next
Priority 01
Resolve WhatsApp attribution
Completing the WhatsApp Business API integration with Meta Business Manager will unlock proper attribution for the Messages campaign. Once Meta can see who actually started a conversation, the campaign will optimise toward real leads. This single action has the highest potential impact on measured CPL.
Priority 02
Scale the Italy creative
The Italy ad generated 361 clicks at 1.03 AED in September. Increasing its budget allocation — and potentially testing a dedicated ad set around it — is the clearest short-term lever available. The creative has demonstrated audience fit; the question now is how much volume it can sustain before saturation.
Priority 03
Pause Italy Arabic · redirect to open ad set
The Italy Arabic ad set closed September with 1 conversation in 29 days at 247 AED CPL — the least efficient spend in the account. Pausing it and redirecting its budget to the open ad set (where Italy Engagement is performing at 1.03 AED/click) recovers spend with no loss of results.
Priority 04
Introduce fresh creative for Maldives
The Maldives ad is generating 32 conversations per month at a stable 34 AED CPL — solid performance. A new creative variant would extend its reach before the current version fatigues, and give the algorithm a fresh signal to work with. The destination resonates; a new angle on it should too.
